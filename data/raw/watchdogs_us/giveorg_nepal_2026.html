Nepal Flood Relief: How to Give Wisely and Support Trusted Charities

August 27, 2026

As Nepal flood relief efforts are underway, thoughtful giving can help turn compassion into meaningful support.

What Happened in the Nepal Floods?

On August 26, 2026, severe flash flooding and landslides struck parts of Nepal and Tibet following intense rainfall and a glacial collapse.

According to the Associated Press, more than 390 people were killed and more than 1,400 are reported missing.

According to CNN, these figures include more than 700 foreigners from two dozen countries, including the United States.

The disaster has caused serious infrastructure damage and left some areas difficult to reach after roads and bridges were damaged or swept away.

Authorities and humanitarian organizations are also monitoring the risk posed by a landslide-created natural dam that could cause additional flooding if it breaches.

How Charities Are Responding to the Nepal Flooding

BBB Accredited Charities have already begun mobilizing in response to the flooding.

Their efforts include supporting search and rescue operations, deploying or supporting medical teams, providing medicines and medical supplies, and delivering emergency food, clean water, shelter materials, hygiene supplies, and other essentials.

They are also assessing longer-term needs, including restoring water and health services, and protecting children and other vulnerable people.

Tips for Donating to Nepal Flood Relief

For those looking to support organizations providing relief in Nepal, here are some tips to help you make wise giving decisions:

  • Look for On-the-Ground Presence. Charities with existing programs and local partners in affected areas are often better positioned to respond quickly and effectively.
  • Understand How Your Donation Will Be Used. Look for appeals that are upfront and clear about what disaster relief services you are supporting.
  • Choose Experienced Organizations. Consider established organizations with experience responding to disasters. Be cautious of newly formed charities that may lack the infrastructure or expertise to deliver aid.
  • Be Cautious With In-Kind Donations.  Donating money is typically the quickest and most effective way to help, giving charities the flexibility to direct resources where they are needed most.
  • Understand Crowdfunding. If considering crowdfunding, it is safest to give to someone you personally know and trust. Review the platform’s policies and procedures, keeping in mind that while some crowdfunding sites take measures to vet campaigns, others don’t.
  • Verify Charity Trustworthiness. You can verify a charity’s trustworthiness by checking whether they meet all 20 BBB Standards for Charity Accountability on Give.org.
